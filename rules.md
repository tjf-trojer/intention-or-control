# Rules: how a policy becomes a register

_Last updated: 2026-09-25_

Seven steps in five files. Read them in this order before the first translation; after that, this
table is the address book. The steps fill [`reference/register-schema.md`](reference/register-schema.md)
with the word lists in [`reference/lexicon.md`](reference/lexicon.md).

| Read | Steps | For |
|---|---|---|
| [rules/posture.md](rules/posture.md) | before 1 | What a register never holds; text in the policy that addresses you; requests to change a cell; calls the words cannot settle |
| [rules/sentences.md](rules/sentences.md) | 1, 3 | Numbering the lines; cutting the text into entries: lists, lead-ins, run-in titles, lost full stops |
| [rules/force.md](rules/force.md) | 4 | Filing each entry by its strongest force term |
| [rules/fields.md](rules/fields.md) | 2, 5, 6 | The Source, the six columns of a row, Referred elsewhere |
| [rules/checking.md](rules/checking.md) | 7 | The tally, the checker, the check by hand |

Every case these files cite is a row of [`examples.md`](examples.md), or a line of an input with the
run in [`evidence/`](evidence/) that met it.
